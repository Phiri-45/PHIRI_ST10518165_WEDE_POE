About PUNYETE

Rooted in Pitori 012 Culture

Created by the "Sosh Plata" hitmaker, PUNYETE is an independent South African streetwear brand that deeply reflects kasi township culture, hip-hop lifestyle, and the energetic creative spirit of Pitori.

Our purpose is to make authentic South African style and street culture accessible to everyone while celebrating local identity, sound, and fashion.

Punyete Sontaga

Within South Africa, PUNYETE drives communities through Punyete Sontaga—a daytime hip-hop and arts-focused event series driven by the brand to bring young creatives together across Pretoria.

Our Purpose is to sustainably make the pleasure and benefits of South African Style accessible to everyone.
